# Support

S is free open source under Apache-2.0 for self-installation. S1.2.2 is
owner-tested and accepted for the documented scope; see
[AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md#testing-status-and-limitations) for successful workflows and
remaining unknowns. There is no personal acknowledgement, response or correction
deadline, monitoring entitlement or personal service obligation. The license's
warranty and liability terms apply, subject to mandatory law.

Sanitized reproducible bug reports may be filed
in `cogni-work/consulting-studio-skills`. Include exact archive/commit and
method/resource versions, actual host/surface, expected/observed behavior, bounded
reader evidence and a fictional reproduction. Keep client/firm/personal data and
credentials out of reports. Suspected vulnerabilities use the private contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Future candidates may be delivered separately as ZIPs. A public release requires
a deliberate maintainer decision; no fixed release cadence is promised. Known
critical fixes should not be withheld solely until a routine release or payment.
Adoption remains the customer's explicit tested action. Third-party platform
changes, integrations, customer modifications, training and data repair do not
create an implied included service. M care and L adaptation are separate offers.
