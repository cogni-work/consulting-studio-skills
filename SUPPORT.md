# Support

S is free open source under Apache-2.0 for self-installation. Release 0.1.0 is
prepared locally; public publication/readback remains pending at this preparation
checkpoint. No qualified host, care entitlement or personal service obligation is
claimed. There is no personal acknowledgement, response or correction deadline.
The license's warranty and liability terms apply, subject to mandatory law.

After publication and readback, sanitized reproducible bug reports may be filed
in `cogni-work/consulting-studio-skills`. Include exact archive/commit and
method/resource versions, actual host/surface, expected/observed behavior, bounded
reader evidence and a fictional reproduction. Keep client/firm/personal data and
credentials out of reports. Suspected vulnerabilities use the private contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Ordinary release batching aims roughly quarterly as capacity permits. Known
critical fixes should not be withheld solely until the next quarter or payment.
Adoption remains the customer's explicit tested action. Third-party platform
changes, integrations, customer modifications, training and data repair do not
create an implied included service. M care and L adaptation are separate offers.
