# Supported pursuit record profile 0.1.5

Use the [event contract](event-contract.md) fields and statuses with the three entry
modes in the [supported input boundary](narrow-pilot-boundary-0.1.5.json). Require
whole permitted source context, original identity, current read scope, rights and
human-note correspondence. Preparation creates no business effect.

Assess proposal acceptance, buyer result, concluded order and current work
coverage separately. An independently proved concluded order and buyer win stay
concluded and won when a later balance or chronology is unproved. Unknown facts
stay unresolved; absence of proof is no denial of a historical event.

For recognized actual acts, knowledge and consumption measurements, compare the
actual fact against every evidenced original issuance, original communication
and authenticated archive preservation time. Authenticate the whole archive
and preserve its original identity and independent envelope time. A later
archive does not repair knowledge claimed before the original communication.
Optional unknown issuance stays unknown. Unknown time zone or precision cannot
prove an ordering. Effective dates, expiry, deadlines and evaluation dates have
their own meaning and are not all actual-fact dates.

Existing exact grant responsibility rules remain required. An unsupported
agreeing role or report adds no proof and does not automatically veto a balance
that is independently valid. Applicable contradictions, denials and revocations
still prevent work coverage. A precisely attributable at-conclusion report can
cover that snapshot without an unsupported later continuity extension; the
later snapshot requires its own current proof.

When a faithful input cannot prove requested work, set work_authority.status to
blocked. Its detail and limitations must explicitly say that proof is unresolved
and human review is required before work; include a settling human review
question. Preserve supplied originals and independently proved history. The
blocked value describes coverage, not a denied or rejected historical event.
Structural, source, digest, schema, read-scope, rights and human-note failures
remain hard errors. A general exception must never produce a valid artifact.

Keep author checks, independent review, actual operations, continuity and human
acceptance separate. Unperformed checks remain `not_run`. Preserve all original
records; fresh assessment changes only its separately identified current output.
