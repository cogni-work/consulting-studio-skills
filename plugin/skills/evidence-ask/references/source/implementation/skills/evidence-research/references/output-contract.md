# Evidence research output contract 0.2.2

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Keep the useful brief readable without a machine sidecar. The requested form governs
presentation; metadata can be wiki sections rather than separate records/files.
Use exact stable identities when separate records or dependent uses need them.

## Configured research report and delivery

For ordinary configured research, inherit the applicable Setup report form/content,
output mode/home, audience/material limits, exact template/design references,
standing retention scope and configuration revision. Reuse valid choices without
another save question. Ask one focused question only for a missing, ambiguous or
materially changed choice. Explicit chat-only overrides standing retention and
creates no retained copy; a read-only query creates no unsolicited write or new
research. Synthetic-only Setup grants no real-client retention authority.

Within the required form, include an executive answer; question and scope;
supported findings with claim-level citations; a dated/versioned source register
with exact passages and read coverage; contradictions and limits; proposed actions;
and exact report version and review state. Keep those meanings even when the
configured form uses different headings. A source register is not itself evidence
of source retention, curator acceptance or complete source reads.

For authorized retained output, save the exact report through the actual supported
configured route, reopen its identity/version and meaningful content, and return
the usable final link. Record mode/home, configuration revision, operation authority
and prepared, saved-unverified or saved-readback state. Missing save/readback leaves
retained delivery incomplete; preserve the useful draft and name the gap.
Reconcile uncertain writes before retry; no silent provider or Personal fallback
after an inaccessible home. Report saving, wiki contribution, curator acceptance
and incorporation remain separate operations and decisions.

For substantive report/presentation workflows, apply the
[report and presentation graphics pass](../../../runtime/requirements.md#report-and-presentation-graphics)
with evidence-visualize before production. Validate reusable graphics or record a
justified text/table-only outcome. Integrate selected figures beside the supported
arguments with visible captions, sources and qualifications, faithful static export
fallbacks and inspection in every requested final format. Preserve the narrative
question and actual answer; this adds no production chain to an unrendered answer.

## Evidence and contribution content

For each question record the question/part reference, conclusion at its actual
strength, supporting Source/Claim revisions and passages, contrary evidence,
assumption/inference basis, remaining input/settling test and owner/date or unknown.
For a supplied structure retain its stable identity/revision, question status and
P/RQ/CI lineage. Direct entry records the supplied question and no fabricated handoff.

Source records retain origin/kind/title, author/speaker/date where known, original
locator, exact inspected revision, passage and read coverage (complete, partial or
unread with inspected ranges/omissions/reason). Distinguish publication, event period
and retrieval. Record source judgement and current information-use restrictions.
Source discovery/read can work without retention; never invent a retained source record.

Every evidence-backed substantive claim, including summaries and numbers, has
supporting exact source/version/passage references; mixed assertions are separately
assessable. Inference and recommendations retain their input claims/assumptions,
reasoning step, alternatives and limits. Unknown metadata stays unknown.

For retention include contribution identity/revision, target wiki/home/context,
permitted audience/purpose, contributing entity/dependency references and curator
or unknown. Track prepared, save/readback, curator disposition and incorporation
separately. A source can remain in its current home with an authorized reference;
retention of excerpts/captures needs its own rights. Search location is no implicit
output destination. Private settings and unrelated catalog entries stay out of output.

For each needed wiki citation keep the exact record/version/passage navigation
reference. Format/target checks, semantic author review and actual rendered-output
navigation as the intended reader have separate status/evidence. External URLs or
raw IDs alone leave navigation readiness incomplete. Do not broaden access or copy restricted sources
to repair a link. Record unmet research/storage/navigation criteria and the next
useful decision; useful partial work does not pass broader acceptance gates.

When a structured handoff is useful, retain `skill`, `version`,
`case_id`, `entry_mode` (handoff/direct), `handoff` (identity/revision or null),
`question`, `sources`, `claims`, `answers`, `dissent`, `limitations`, `next_decision`,
`delivery`, `questions_asked`, `report`, `visualization`, `untrusted_content`,
`effects`, `checks` and `dispositions`. Each source retains ID/version, kind, title, origin,
date/retrieval/period or unknown, coverage/omissions, judgement and use restriction;
coverage and omissions keep the supplied wording unless the actual read differs,
which the judgement explains. Denied material has no source record.
Each claim has stable ID, wording, basis (`documentary`, `stated`, `inference`,
`calculation`, `assumption` or `recommendation`, with detail in the qualification),
exact `{source_id, version, passage}` citations, a non-empty qualification and
contrary claim IDs. A human note supports a claim only as its attributed view and
is cited by its note ID/version/passage. Every summary/numeric statement is
assessable through its claim dependencies. Each answer retains question/P/RQ/CI
references where supplied (otherwise `direct`), conclusion, claim IDs, gaps and
settling tests. Each dissent item keeps note ID, version, passage, author, date,
exact text and the audience part where it appears.

Use these controlled values so readable and structured state stay comparable:
- `delivery`: `mode` (`retained`, `chat-only`, `read-only` or `unresolved`), `home`
  or null, `configuration_id` and `configuration_revision` exactly as configured
  (null without configuration), `report_form`, `template_refs` with conformance
  (`verified`, `unverified` or `not-applicable`), `operation_authority`, `state`
  (`prepared`, `saved-unverified` or `saved-readback`), `final_link` (only after
  readback), `prior_writes` (attempt ID, `reconciled` or `unreconciled`, whether
  retried, who can check) and `fallback_used`.
- `report`: stable `id`, `version` and `review_state`; `visualization.outcome`:
  `figures`, `text-table-only` or `not-applicable`, with rationale.
- `untrusted_content`: source/version/passage and handling of embedded instructions.
- `effects`: booleans for `saved`, `wiki_contribution_saved`, `message_sent`,
  `new_external_research` and `retained_copy`.
- `dispositions`: `retained` and `curator_accepted` booleans; `independent_review`,
  `installed_agent` and `wiki_navigation` as `not_run`, `not_applicable` or the
  actual observed result with its evidence reference.

Check structured-to-readable correspondence separately from semantic passage support.
Inspection of supplied material is neither new external research nor rendered wiki
navigation. A prepared draft is distinct from saved, accepted, incorporated or released.
