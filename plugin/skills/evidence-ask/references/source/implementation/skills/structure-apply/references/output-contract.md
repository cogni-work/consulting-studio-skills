# Framework application output contract 0.1.0

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Return the answer or unresolved limit first, then the applied tree/table, supporting
evidence, dissent/notes and next useful human decision. A useful application needs
no fabricated sale, engagement, approval or complete firm profile.

Retain the exact selected method(s), chosen/rejected status and decision source,
readable content/version/digest, applicability and information-use basis. Identify
Problem Structure identity/revision and input status, original question, scope,
cutting principle, stable P/RQ/CI IDs, settling tests and combination rule. Supplied
material can satisfy the input meanings without an upstream skill run. Label any
synthetic material explicitly and do not claim upstream execution from its presence.

For every branch retain its proposition, method rule/test, observed exact evidence
and contrary evidence, derivation, supported/refuted/open status, result cell,
implication and next needed input (owner/date or unknown). A refuted proposition has
an affirmative evidentiary basis; an open cell is empty, not zero, a guessed answer
or a recommendation disguised as evidence. Assumption cells are distinctly labeled
with source/owner and uncertainty. Define numeric unit/population/period/denominator
and comparability; never manufacture a total or ranking from incompatible measures.

The combination implication states its basis and scope, not a business decision.
Keep attributed statements, documentary observations, analyst inference, alternative
explanations, dissent, human notes, omissions and uncertainty visible. Exact Result
references can remain sections of the application; downstream consult-results or
narrative-shape consumes the question, chosen method, exact evidence, statuses,
implication, limits, decisions and dependencies without treating the draft as adopted.

An optional structured sidecar retains `skill`, `procedure_version`, `contract_version`,
`mode` (`default`), `case_id`, `synthetic`, `input_sha256`, `structure_ref`,
`method_refs`, `choice`, `branches`, `implication`, `scope`, `human_originals`,
`source_coverage`, `unresolved`, `dependencies`, `checks` and `effects`. Each branch
has `id`, `rq_ids`, `ci_ids`, `proposition`, `method_rule`, `status`, `cell` (null for
open), `basis`, `evidence` (source/revision/digest/passage/quote), `derivation`,
`implication`, `needed_inputs`. These meanings do not prescribe a platform schema.

A blocked application names the missing required method/version or denied use and
settling condition, leaves its branches unapplied and claims no method application.
It may return a permitted independent evidence inventory without exporting denied
method content. General work continues only where independent and authorized.

Correction records bind old/new byte identities, exact affected uses/checks, proposed
rechecks, incomplete dependency coverage and preservation digests. They propose
effects; storage occurrence or saved stale flags need separate authorized evidence.
The current draft and prior/issued versions have separate references. Late human
notes stay exact. No check survives an affected source/method/output change merely
because the locator or version label is unchanged.

Author checks distinguish reference format/target correspondence, semantic support,
readability and actual intended-reader wiki navigation. Independent review, human
review/release, actual workflow execution, continuity/collaboration and real-client
readiness are separate dispositions. A prepared file alone establishes none of these.
