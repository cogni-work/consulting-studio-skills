# Evidence visualization requirements

| Operational reference | Apply in this method |
| --- | --- |
| [Common requirements](../../../runtime/requirements.md) | Explore or explain directly from permitted supplied evidence. Preserve ownership/use restrictions, human originals, dissent, uncertainty and exact source passage coverage. |
| [Output contract](output-contract.md) | Retain aggregate and relevant segments, exact values/derivation, measurement/coding definitions, scope, encoding rationale and a complete substantive claim ledger. |
| [Binding contract](../../../workspace-bindings/contract.md) and [binding catalog](../../../runtime/binding-catalog.json) | Resolve source/input/output roles, current identity, audience/use and permissions separately. Supplied material needs no unrelated discovery or setup. |

Preserve exact content/evidence/design handoff and all substantive claim support. |

Define numerator, denominator, unit, population, period, baseline, metric version,
missingness, uncertainty and transformations before comparing. Weight ratios by
their actual denominators and inspect material segment reversals. Keep observations,
estimates, assumptions and models distinct. A descriptive decomposition or association
does not establish cause, business impact or owner adoption.

Reject duplicate row and claim identities, including collisions with derived claim
IDs; distinct coordinates do not justify identity reuse. Preserve all observed cells,
incompatible comparisons and missing/zero-denominator limits. Unsupported area/volume
encodings need a faithful proposed alternative and an owner decision when consequential.

Use attributed qualitative maps without scores when no coding method is supplied.
Quantification requires exact coverage, denominator, assumptions and limits; sample
counts establish no population prevalence. A neutral note headline reflects whether
coding exists, without inventing agreement or disagreement. Include any substantive
summary and its supporting note passages in the claim ledger.

Read exact supplied prior specifications and issued captions/notes before planning
corrections. Include their identities in affected uses; distinguish caption inspection
from rendered inspection and uninspected homes. Proposed impacts persist no flags.
Use publishing-render for requested production through actual authorized capabilities
and publishing-inspect for the exact artifact. Keep semantic support, arithmetic,
rendering, intended-reader wiki navigation, independent review and acceptance separate.
