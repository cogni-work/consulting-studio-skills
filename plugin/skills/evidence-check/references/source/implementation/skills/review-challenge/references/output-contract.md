# Review-challenge output contract 0.1.0

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Input: exact permitted Proposal/Deliverable, exact compatible evidence-check result,
stated criteria, audience/purpose/use basis and permitted human originals. Supplied
input and source excerpts are valid direct entry; invocation of
[evidence-check](../../../../../../SKILL.md) is optional.
Missing exact draft/check/criteria stops the affected challenge and returns useful
bounded questions. Supplied policy/criteria govern; geography/industry alone implies
no local law. This is read-only preparation, not a binding API or enforcement layer.

Return a readable challenge. When a structured companion is useful, retain:

- `skill`, `version`, `case_id`, `mode`, `identity`: exact governing method and
  input identities, source/criteria
  and input digests when present, exact draft/check references and content digests,
  context and target. Label synthetic applicability; a target label supplies no
  owner adoption.
- `summary`: decision-changing answer/limit with qualifications. `coverage`: one
  row per supplied criterion with disposition (objection/strength/unresolved/not_applicable),
  linked objection IDs or a substantive reason. `strengths`: supported positive
  arguments and their limits; challenges are not an automatic negative verdict.
- `objections`: unique ID, criterion IDs, exact `draft_units`, exact `check_units`,
  simulated `perspective` (buyer/finance-director/delivery-reviewer), `question`,
  `finding`, `basis` (contradicted/unsupported/contingent/unresolved), `consequence`,
  exact `evidence` references, `counterargument` with evidence and limitation,
  `proposed_action`, `settling_input`, responsible supplied role or null, and
  `due` (supplied value or null). No invented numeric confidence or urgency.
- Evidence: `{source_id, revision, passage, quote}` matching permitted exact source
  excerpts. Check references identify original check unit IDs and their verdicts;
  they are not independent support. Citation correspondence, passage support and
  actual intended-reader navigation are separate; source quality/read omissions remain visible.
- `alternatives`: candidate, rationale, tradeoff, settling_test and proposal status.
  Each alternative remains hypothetical unless an attributable act establishes adoption.
- `commitments`: exact existing declarations and their status/source; distinguish
  draft promises from confirmed acts, unknowns and proposed corrections. A proposed
  owner role is not an appointed individual. Do not create business acts or dates.
- `human_originals`: verbatim permitted originals, including late dissent; they
  remain outside claim inventory, and are not exposed to a wider audience.
- `resumption`: exact prior/check/source links, changed scope and stale-check/recheck
  disposition or explicit initial-run status. Old receipts stay fixed.
- `gaps`: coverage, missing evidence/authority/capability and next settling conditions.
  `review`: author_self_check performed; independent_review/human_review_of_record/
  human_acceptance not_run; named_human_reviewer supplied or null. Simulated role
  challenge is assistant preparation, including when a supplied reviewer is named.
- `effects`: draft_modified, retained_live, sent, approved, released, commitments_changed,
  native_executed, research_invoked and scheduled all false. `native`: integration,
  runtime, intended_reader_navigation, current_target_adoption, owner_acceptance all
  NOT_RUN. Actual operation, adoption and acceptance require their own evidence.

Readable output exposes the findings, check identities, source quotes, coverage,
counterarguments, alternatives, proposals, unknown dates/owners, dissent and limits.
Proposed edits do not replace original content. Identity and correspondence checks establish
only their observed scope;
they do not independently certify all reasoning.
An unfamiliar-reader self-check records whether the answer/limit, material
qualification and action can be found; it establishes no human comprehension test.
