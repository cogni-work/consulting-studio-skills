# Consult-planning output contract 0.1.1

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Use one default mode. Related business objects may be sections of a readable plan;
this vocabulary does not mandate a host schema or database. Inputs from
[consult-framing](../../consult-framing/references/output-contract.md) or compatible
permitted agreed framing are valid without a forced invocation chain.

## Input

Bind the ordinary request, assessment, audience/use basis and exact readable framing.
Retain the framing sections question, scope, dates, success, acceptance, outcomes,
methods, stakeholders, knowledge, planning_inputs, risks, assumptions and unresolved,
with their value/status/support. Keep record identity, owner/home, exact source
revisions/passages/coverage, human originals, promise/handoff and primary/supplementary/
historical authority identifiable. Check compatible supplied framing against these
business meanings; label synthetic inputs as synthetic.

Optional observations identify exact input, part/question, supported/refuted/open
status, method applicability, evidenced assignments, due dates and actual receipts.
A chosen method must be readable, authorized and applicable to the proposed work;
a framing reference alone supplies no analysis method. Unknown information stays
unknown. Private/denied sources do not enter output.

## Output

Lead with practical next work, blockers and decisions. Preserve baseline, record,
promise, handoff, authority snapshot, human originals and all qualifications by exact
reference. The plan has its own ID/revision and a `prepared_unretained`, `proposed`
disposition until actual authorized adoption or retention is established. Include:

- Exact method/version, request/mode, input/content identities and synthetic status
  where applicable. A demonstration target supplies no actual adoption.
- Work items: stable IDs, grouping/part, title, proposed/agreed basis, owner or unknown,
  separate proposed role, state, dates/status, dependencies, next action, expected
  result and exact baseline criteria.
- Client inputs: what/owner/date/status, next action and consequence for named work
  and milestone. Unknown receipt dates never become confirmed delivery.
- Milestones: exact commitment reference, due date and basis, linked work/proposed
  deliverable, unchanged acceptance criteria/procedure, status, forecast and delay
  consequence. Authority expiry is distinct from a delivery commitment.
- Risks/issues/assumptions/dependencies: source, owner or unknown, proposed role,
  impact, next action/date and settling evidence; preserve existing entries.
- Proposed decisions: actual relevant decider role, exact subject and date or unknown;
  a proposal is not a decision. Outcomes remain expected until separately observed.
- Consumed sources with exact revision/passage/quote/digest/link, and lineage from
  framing/source/observation/method/original to known uses, correction consequences
  and unknown external coverage.
- Actual reader findings or labeled simulation, author source/criterion checks and
  local citation correspondence. Independent review, intended-reader navigation,
  human usefulness, actual runtime/continuity and real-client acceptance each have
  their own exact-version evidence; leave unperformed checks `NOT_RUN`.

Structured companions may retain `baseline`, `record`, `promise`, `handoff`,
`authority_snapshot`, `human_originals`, `work_items`, `client_inputs`, `milestones`,
`raid`, `decisions`, `sources`, `lineage`, identity/check/disposition fields and exact
content digests. This is compatible representation, not an implied storage operation.

Assignments/dates/dependencies/groupings stay proposed unless supported. An agreed
commitment cannot be downgraded to a proposal; a guessed role cannot become an agreed
assignment. Preparing the plan adopts no new criterion or method/settings and makes
no work-start, send, release, acceptance or provider-write claim. Actual approval
belongs to the lead; commitment changes belong to sponsor and contracting/firm authority.

## Receipt and assignment fidelity

Reconcile received inputs for receipt/coverage rather than request them again.
Missing lists and unverified reported descriptions remain distinct; request only
identified gaps. On resumption retain the original narrow receipt-tracking assignment
and due date, while reconciling completion/current applicability. New coverage-review
or chase work remains proposed/unassigned. Assignment evidence is not a renewed grant.
Preserve issued plans, earlier findings, exact old/new content identities and human
originals; changed input bytes invalidate affected checks even at unchanged labels.

Check for invented commitments, lost scope/authority/exclusions, wrong passages,
refuted evidence promoted, omitted local exceptions/notes, unread required methods,
hidden unknowns and stale dependencies. Source correspondence establishes neither
independent truth nor actual intended-reader access. Inspect current records and
uncertain effects before retry; do not replace an unreadable authoritative home.
