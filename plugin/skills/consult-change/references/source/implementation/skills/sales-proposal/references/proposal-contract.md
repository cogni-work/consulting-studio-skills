# Proposal contract 0.1.0

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

A business proposal is a versioned promise owned by the pursuit owner, with
commercial approval by its actual approver. Ordinary work may use sections of a
short letter. Use these structured fields when an exact handoff is useful, with
matching readable content. They prescribe no platform API or storage operation.

## Useful form and exact lineage

Lead with the supported engagement promise or decisive unresolved limit. Respect
requested section titles/order, including mandatory tender forms. Show practical
options, evidence and restrictions at their actual strength. Keep decision-changing
exclusions, unresolved team/fee/timing, supplied responsibility conflicts and dissent readable.
Preserve all exact buyer criteria, IDs, wording, statuses, scores, conditions and
source revisions. Every option states scope/outcome, approach, team/skills/status,
duration, dates, client inputs/owners, fees/terms/assumptions, exclusions and acceptance.
A proposed acceptance procedure is a proposal, never client agreement.

Every substantive evidenced claim carries exact source/version/passage, quote,
coverage and authorized browsable source-record link. Local links establish
correspondence only; keep rendered intended-reader navigation `not_run` until tested.
Semantic support, arithmetic and source-specific readability need separate checking.
Do not copy restricted evidence to repair navigation or include private dissent in a
buyer-facing section. An internal review annex preserves permitted human originals.

## Structured handoff output

Top-level fields: `skill`, `version`, `case_id`, `synthetic`, `target`, `input_sha256`,
`lead`, `entry_mode`, `proposal`, `bindings`, `criteria`, `upstream_context`,
`planning_inputs`, `input_read_basis`, `human_originals`, `sources_read`, `claims`, `options`,
`criteria_coverage`, `proof_assessments`, `collisions`, `discount_assessment`,
`correction_flags`, `method_lanes`, `questions`, `challenge`, `review`, `limitations`,
`payload_review`, `route_notes`, `dispositions`, `actions_performed`.

- Identity uses the exact sales-proposal method, case and target. `input_sha256`
  lists every source, contract and input artifact actually supplied.
  `bindings` repeats exact case bindings, including JSON and readable artifact
  hashes/paths, namespace, brief/proof IDs/revisions, criteria digest, upstream
  source identity and optional invocation status. Direct inputs bind supplied
  source bytes, never a fabricated upstream artifact. `upstream_context` repeats
  the contract-preserved fields from each exact artifact, including rights and
  both human-note schemas. Original history is immutable.
- `entry_mode`: proposal or authorization_gap. `proposal` is `id`, new `revision`,
  `status: draft`, exact `form`, `sections` (ordered `id`, `title`, assistant-written
  `content`), `prepared_without` and `release_state: pending human review`.
  `prepared_without` repeats the case's absent research/formal-scoping inputs.
  A direct-award/internal exception has null proposal, no offer options and
  `route_notes` preserving each supplied route and its authorization/criteria gaps.
- `input_read_basis`: exact current scoped internal read basis, separate from outgoing rights.
- `planning_inputs`: entire supplied planning record unchanged. `options`: new `id`,
  nonempty `scope`, `outcome`, `approach`, `team`, `schedule`, `client_inputs`,
  `price`, `terms`, `exclusions`, `acceptance`, `feasibility`, `criterion_ids`.
  Cover the supplied real alternative effort scenarios; each option binds one supplied `effort_id`; arithmetic uses its exact role/days
  and supplied currency/rate record. Planning effort is a stated estimate.
  `team` records supplied `member_id`, exact `role`, `skills`, `commitment`,
  `availability` and assistant-written `implication`; it never promotes pending
  commitment/availability. `schedule` repeats supplied `start`, `end`,
  `duration_workdays` and adds `dependencies`; dates are proposed, not agreed.
  `client_inputs` and `terms` retain the exact supplied objects, including unknowns.
  `price` has `currency`, `lines` (`role`, `days`, `rate_id`, `day_rate`, `amount`),
  `known_subtotal`, `total` (null if any rate is unknown), `status`
  (indicative/placeholder), `assumptions`, `missing_basis`. No unsupported fixed fee.
  `acceptance` has `criteria`, `procedure`, `authority_status: proposed`.
  `feasibility` has `status` conditional/proposed, `conditions`, `settling_step`.
- `collisions`: one per supplied known dated overlap for the same proposed team
  member, with exact planning/other IDs, commitment kind (contingent/confirmed),
  overlap start/end and assistant-written implication. Unknown dated intervals get
  `limitations`; no capacity completeness or invented availability.
  `discount_assessment`: exact supplied request (or null), `status: proposed` or
  not_requested, nonempty `tradeoff` when requested, and pending human approver.
  Discounted figures are not part of a new approved fee; no invented margin data.
- `claims`: `id`, `text`, `basis` (documentary/stated/inferred/proposed), `support`
  (exact source_id/revision/passage/quote/href), `qualification`. Inferences cite
  inputs and explain the inferential step. Proposed promises cite planning basis;
  they never assert delivered results. Unsupported marketing asks are explicitly
  rejected/unresolved in proof assessments, not repeated as credible proof.
- `sources_read`: every exact supplied permitted local source used, with source_id,
  revision, coverage, omissions. Upstream artifact lineage remains separately bound.
  `proof_assessments`: one per case `proof_request`, exact `request`,
  `rights` (exact grant plus current_state/use_status), `disposition`
  permitted/blocked/unresolved/unsupported, `support`, `relevance`, `limitations`,
  `settling_step`. All-owner current proposal-use grants are separate from historical
  upstream grants. Requested unsupported outcomes remain unsupported even if reuse
  rights permit the comparison excerpt. Blocked proof has no buyer-facing inclusion.
- `criteria_coverage`: one per exact criterion with entire `criterion`, response
  `section_ids`, `status` addressed/partial/uncovered and source-specific `detail`.
  Uncovered mandatory criteria go into limitations. Tender extras preserve exact
  critical dates, clarifications, CV permission records and pitch request from form.
- `human_originals`: union of exact permitted input originals, preserving distinct
  brief objects (`id`, revision, passage, author, text, href), proof objects
  (`source_ref`, author, text, href) and direct source originals. Either list order
  is valid; all fields and every revision must survive. No schema projection.
  `correction_flags`: every supplied correction unchanged plus assistant-written
  `affected_uses` and `status: stale`; never rewrite historical artifacts.
- `method_lanes`: exact required reference/lane, blocked status and attributed
  settling step for absent method. Common drafting may continue without substituting
  or claiming application. `questions`: source-specific question, settles, owner,
  date, basis. Ask only what changes this proposal; no generic profile/budget intake.
- `challenge`: each of need/urgency/alternatives/value/feasibility/price with question,
  source-specific response_or_gap and basis. `review`: exact supplied reviewer/
  commercial roles, `status: pending`, `independence: not_established`; AI challenge
  never counts as human approval. Exception routes have no sales challenge. Same-person review remains honest when supplied.
- `payload_review`: exact inventory for text/names/links/metadata/comments/attachments/
  receipts with assistant-written assessment and `status: draft_only`; supplied
  restricted internal notes/proof remain excluded from buyer-facing sections.
  `limitations` contains all material evidence/method/commercial/authority gaps and
  actual checks still owed. Readable Markdown includes the draft in form/order,
  option meaning, claims/qualifications, tender coverage, challenge, questions,
  limitations, and an internal annex with the exact human originals.
- `dispositions`: retained/transferred/shared/submitted/offer_adopted/contract_concluded/
  order_created/work_authorized/research_invoked/upstream_invoked are false;
  wiki_navigation/original_source_inspection/native_access_enforcement/installed_agent/
  collaboration_continuity/independent_review/human_usefulness/real_client_readiness
  are not_run in a draft-only profile until separately checked. `actions_performed`
  is empty. Record actual checks separately; author checks remain separate from
  independent review and human usefulness.

## Governing requirements and limits

Apply [journey stages 2–4](../../../runtime/requirements.md#business-lifecycle),
[Proposal and Planning Assumption meanings](../../../runtime/requirements.md#business-records),
[knowledge boundaries](../../../runtime/requirements.md#evidence-and-knowledge) and
[decisions](../../../runtime/requirements.md#human-decisions).
Keep source fidelity, actual planning evidence, tender form, CV/reuse permission,
sceptical challenge and exact-version human approval visible. A geography, sector
or service label establishes no law or firm mandate. Buyer briefs and proof
selections provide exact interfaces, never new approval, permissions or a mandatory
workflow.
