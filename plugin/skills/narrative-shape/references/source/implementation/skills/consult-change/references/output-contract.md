Use one default mode. Compatible permitted
[framing](../../consult-framing/references/output-contract.md) replaces upstream

preserve exact decisions and lineage; they do not define a host API, transactional

## Input and assessment

Bind ordinary request, assessment time, reader/purpose/use basis, exact agreed
Baseline and primary authorization, identified change and proposed patch/revision.
Read attributable five-dimensional assessment, objections and human notes; scope,
cost, amendment and lead-adoption acts with exact baseline/proposal references;
optional supplementary grants, current reporter responsibility and actual usage.
Keep source identity/revision/author/passage/home/coverage and actual content digests.
A general actor label supplies no authorization for another engagement/change.

Assess scope, time, cost, quality and outcomes. Do not silently ignore an unsupported
proposed edit: keep that part blocked with its settling input. Label synthetic acts,
relative exercise days and stand-ins; none supplies actual business authority.

## Output

Return a useful Scope Change brief with stable identity/revision and actual
preparation disposition (`prepared_unretained` for an unsaved draft). Preserve the
original baseline/order/notes, exact proposal and assessment, separate scope/cost/
adoption states, exact effective baseline when evidenced, linked concluded amendment,
supplementary bounds/usage/responsibility, current requested-work coverage, historical
at-risk work, contrary evidence, human originals and settling decisions. Preparing
this brief effects no adoption, provider operation, retention, send or stale flag.
Leave unexercised review/operation checks `NOT_RUN`.

Current original authority is assessed at the actual time and cannot be extended by
a view. Distinguish no-commercial, absorbed, priced, pending/provisional, refused,
lapsed and withdrawn treatments. Original work continues only within its current
original authority. Added-work expiry neither cancels nor extends that basis;
suspension/cancellation stops new work. Infer no adopted standard, client payment,
acceptance, causality, savings or achieved outcome.

## Exact subjects and chronology

Keep the Baseline ID stable. Reusing a target revision label requires explicit
correction/event identity and distinct content fingerprints. In a structured record,
retain `revision_event_id`, `corrects_sha256` for original baseline content and
`content_sha256` for distinct candidate content. Exact proposal hashes bind these
fields to decisions. Ambiguous changed content blocks dependent adoption.

Retain every earlier adopted change in the current exact baseline; a later pending,
refused or lapsed change does not erase it or its cost authority. Preserve the
earliest order and previous baselines separately. Request, scope, cost, amendment
and adoption form an evidenced partial order. Same-day prerequisites need explicit
sequence evidence; compare all supplied prerequisites through adoption. Use actual
source dates rather than a fixed exercise day. Do not invent a clock or backdate acts.

Evaluate cost coverage at adoption's effective time. Later expiry can leave valid
adopted content intact while current added-work coverage is STOP. Keep this separate
in `current_cost_coverage` when using structured records. Priced activation requires
scope approval, concluded amendment and lead adoption; absorbed activation requires
firm cost/risk approval and reason. No-commercial activation still needs evidenced
assessment, scope approval and lead adoption.

## Bounded supplementary work

A supplementary grant binds exact `baseline`/`proposal_sha256`, or an explicit
`own_risk_basis` with exact original baseline identity/content, `work_sha256` for the
complete added-work list, and `independent_of_proposal: true`. It covers only the
firm's bounded own-risk work; it creates no client commitment or baseline adoption.
Earlier bounded authority need not postdate the request. Changed work/baseline bounds
leave coverage unresolved and STOP. Record approver, risk bearer, allowed work,
limits and expiry separately from current usage and reporter responsibility.

Usage is cumulative at the stated measurement time. Keep all reports and at-risk
history; later responsible totals may increase. Numerically agreeing unsupported
reports add no proof and erase no valid current evidence. An additional future
appointment does not cancel established current responsibility. Competing totals,
unexplained decreases, malformed/unknown consumption, applicable denial/revocation,
expiry or exhaustion leave dependent coverage unresolved and stop new work. A report
grants no extension. Show remaining bounds only when supported and the requested
increment fits; exhaustion/expiry requires a finite extend/stop decision.

## Correction and review

Reconstruct every permitted whole decision fact—people, state, source, timing,
exact subjects and limits—rather than trust an approval flag. Check readable output
against complete sources and criteria, retaining exact old/new inputs and failed
checks. Preserve original reports, issued work, decisions and human originals;
changed bytes invalidate affected support/checks, with unread homes disclosed.
An unavailable read leaves business consequences unknown unless observations
establish them. Inspect uncertain effects before retry and never duplicate a save.
Author/reader simulations, passage correspondence, intended-reader navigation,
independent review and actual human decisions retain separate exact-version evidence.
