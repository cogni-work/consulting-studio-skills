# Operational requirements for changed work

| Requirement | Apply it in the assessment |
| --- | --- |
| [Change journey](../../../runtime/requirements.md#business-lifecycle) | Assess scope, time, cost, quality and outcomes; distinguish no-commercial, absorbed, priced, pending/provisional and terminal treatments. |
| [Decisions and responsibility](../../sales-pursuit-event/references/time-authority-model-0.1.15.md) | Separate scope, cost, lead adoption and current responsible usage. An approving role alone proves no reporter responsibility or current balance. |
| [Delivery objects](../../../runtime/requirements.md#business-records) | Keep stable baseline/change identities, original order and linked amendments, exact proposal subjects and effective history. |
| [Framing contract](../../consult-framing/references/output-contract.md) | Compatible agreed framing and authorization suffice; no mandatory sale or upstream invocation. |
| [Knowledge and lineage](../../../runtime/entity-lineage.md) | Preserve whole decisions, contrary evidence, human originals, same-label corrections and partial dependency coverage. |
| [Status contract](../../consult-status/references/output-contract.md) | Missing permission/read blocks coverage; business and milestone consequences stay unknown unless actual observations establish them. |

An earlier absorbed change stays part of the current exact baseline when a later
priced change is pending, refused or lapsed. Check request, scope, pricing,
amendment and adoption in their evidenced partial order; same-day acts need explicit
order. Assess cost coverage at effective adoption time separately from current
permission for added work. Later expiry can stop new work without erasing valid
historical adoption. Supplementary own-risk bounds grant neither client commitment
nor baseline activation; exhaustion or expiry requires the finite extend/stop choice.

Read only the references needed for the selected task. Apply their current adopted
requirements within the actual audience, purpose and available authorized operations.
A method dependency names compatible information; it does not require a prior skill
invocation. Retain exact source versions and content identities when consuming outputs.
