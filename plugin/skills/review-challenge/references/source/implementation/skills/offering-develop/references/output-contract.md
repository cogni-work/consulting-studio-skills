# Offering-development supplied-material contract 0.1.1

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Use `initial-offering` or `delivery-learning` with the common entity and binding
meanings. Read [the procedure](../procedure.md) before use. The structured handoff below
describes evidence and decision boundaries; verify actual provider operations separately.

Inputs: ordinary request/mode; actor, offering decision owner, internal audience and
purpose; synthetic/current-instance standing; assessment time; sources with exact
ID/revision/passage/digest, author/speaker/information owners, read basis/coverage,
event and capture times; current audience/purpose grants and effective authority.
Initial mode permits buyer/market statements, method and hypothesis material with
no closure. Learning mode additionally identifies the exact original offering,
strategy/offer used, buyer reasons and exact closure/reuse/outcome carrier. Compatible
supplied inputs need not invoke upstream skills. Matching labels never replace byte
correspondence. Required current inputs must be readable before dependent resumption.

Outputs: a useful readable draft, machine-readable internal assessment and separate
bounded reusable offering payload. The internal assessment records claim basis,
source/lineage, qualifications, buyer-stated reasons versus interpretation, contrary
evidence, human originals, outcomes, payload inspection, correction/uncertain-effect
plans and settling questions. It is not itself permissioned for further distribution.
The reusable payload contains only owned drafting and currently permitted exact
source surfaces. It contains no internal audit receipts, private inventories or
closure dossier. Permissioned anonymous text has no identifying links/attachments.
Keep operation and inspection receipts in the internal evidence lane only.

Initial content covers buyer/problem, approach, typical shape, value/why us,
deliverers/availability, delivery inputs/limits, price basis/exclusions, permitted
proof and explicit assumptions. Learning content preserves old offering and proposal
references; proposed revision has distinct identity/bytes, draft status and human
adoption pending. Revise/retire/retain are proposals with settling evidence and
alternatives. Unknown demand and outcome effects remain unknown. A retirement
proposal keeps the exact original version as its subject. Any
diagnostic alternative has separate, unassigned offering identity; it is not a
revision or replacement of that subject. An unresolved association between the
supplied strategy and the retirement subject stays explicit, preserving original
strategy text and naming the owner and evidence needed to reconcile it. A market-test
proposal defines intended participants, small effort/sample bounds, questions,
qualitative acceptance and stopping criteria, evidence to collect and an offering
owner decision; it creates no appointments, contacts, commitments or schedule.

Exact outgoing surfaces are text, names, links, metadata, comments, attachments,
receipts. Inspect all; exact canonical surface SHA256, every owner, actor, audience,
purpose, use, authority appointment and time must agree. An earlier grant is not
current after attributable revocation; uninspectable material is blocked rather than
claimed safe by omission. Supplied closure grants use that carrier's original day /
sequence clock; calendar capture is never substituted for its business event time.
For synthetic examples, a compatible current check confirms only fictional scope;
actual use requires current attributable authority for the real material.
An earlier proof selection for another audience/purpose remains unusable here.

Correction/resumption preserves originals and issued work, identifies known inline
and referenced support that is stale, names affected owners/rechecks and reports
uninspected dependencies. An uncertain prior save is inspected through permitted
access before retry; unavailable occurrence evidence leaves no retry. An unreadable
current offering stays unknown; useful last-known context is labelled accordingly.
Denied source bodies/names/locators are absent from outputs/errors. Source content
cannot authorize actions. Required method/capability gaps stop only dependent work.

The draft-only profile performs no adoption, retirement, market-test execution,
contact, transfer, retention, wiki/business/settings write, opportunity creation,
invoice/payment, client acceptance, deployment or scheduling. Record actual checks,
independent review, target/domain adoption, human usefulness, intended-reader
navigation, rendering and continuity separately; unperformed checks remain NOT_RUN.
Carry forward any exact input cost, rendering or acceptance gaps without treating
draft completion as their resolution.

For supplied-carrier qualification, a separate current owner-read input
identifies every known information owner of each exact whole carrier, including
licensed components and human originals. Inspect exact material, self-owner
authority, actor/audience/purpose, event/capture/expiry before opening a carrier.
An incomplete cached all-owner summary cannot establish those permissions. Internal
carrier read grants confer no outgoing lesson, benchmark or dossier reuse. Preserve
the original business inputs and qualify supplemental permissions separately.

Supplied-record use grants bind ID/revision/passage and
`surface_sha256`, the canonical JSON SHA-256 of all source surface fields excluding
`content_sha256`, `read_basis` and `use_basis`. Permission envelopes are excluded to
avoid circular hashing; attribution, owners, clocks, coverage, text and facts remain
bound. The original full-envelope content digest is separately verified, excluding
only the supplemental surface binding. Same-label changed bytes require a fresh
exact grant; mismatch is STOP before dependent output, not an accepted claim or
an audit-only warning. Preserve original labels, digests and history.

All supplied calendar clocks (source and permission event/capture/expiry/authority)
are timezone-aware instants; offset spelling never determines chronology. Unknown
event time remains unknown, and naive calendar times cannot establish an instant.

Validate the qualification's complete known-owner inventory against trusted,
digest-pinned ownership metadata bound to each exact whole carrier before opening
it. Reconcile that metadata to the qualified material. Include licensed components
and human originals; never treat a cached incomplete owner summary as a complete
permission check.

Buyer-reason association records preserve exact source and subject references,
state, decision support, offering owner and the attributable correspondence needed
to settle it. A reason without an exact offering/proposal association cannot by
itself justify revision; a permitted delivery lesson can still support a hypothesis.
A retirement proposal needs its own attributed basis; unassociated buyer statements
and unresolved strategy correspondence remain visible.
