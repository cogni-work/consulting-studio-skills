# Shared entity references and correction contract 1.0.0

Use this contract with the [working requirements](requirements.md#business-records).
Keep logical objects as sections of one useful document where appropriate. Use
actual authorized record operations; verify access, saves and readback separately.

## References and skill handoffs

A reference identifies the entity type, stable business identity, exact revision
and content digest where available. Keep owner, author, authoritative home,
audience/use basis and precise version/passage locator distinguishable. Relocation
preserves business identity; the authorized route verifies the new locator and
affected navigation. A repeated revision label cannot hide changed bytes.

A skill handoff carries the smallest useful permitted inputs: question/purpose,
audience, exact entity and evidence references, dependencies, qualifications and
unknowns, effective method/settings references, proposed work and actual operation
and decision dispositions. Carry the resolved delivery mode, authoritative output
home, configuration revision and actual preparation/save/readback state under
[Deliverable mode and home](storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home).
The receiving skill reuses these choices and checks compatibility and actual
read coverage. An upstream skill need not have run when supplied inputs satisfy
the same contract. A reference certifies no access, factual truth or approval.

Audience briefs, storylines and graphic specifications are prepared work products
or sections. Give them separate identity only when versioning, correction or reuse
requires it. Retain human originals at their authoritative home; handoffs use exact
references and permitted excerpts rather than mandatory whole-payload copying.

Distinguish dependency relations such as evidence support, derivation, incorporated
content and rendering from contextual associations such as stakeholder or related
pursuit. Only actual content/evidence dependencies propagate a correction.

## Responsibility and authorized correction

1. The source/knowledge owner or authorized contributor records the exact corrected
   revision, prior revision, reason and supporting evidence through the current
   permitted update route. A new source fact and its storage occurrence are separate.
2. Resolve known dependent revisions within authorized homes and actual coverage.
   Include inline results and any identity-preserving promoted result. Preserve
   the original passage/revision link. Do not search inaccessible homes or expand
   access to discover the whole graph.
3. Prepare each affected synthesis, result, narrative, graphic and artifact's stale
   disposition and the checks invalidated by the change. The responsible content
   owner authorizes and records applicable updates; verify actual readback before
   claiming persisted flags. Read-only asks/checks report detected staleness and
   required work without modifying records.
4. Keep issued artifacts, submitted promises, acceptance decisions and human
   originals fixed. Their owner decides notification, revision, release or other
   consequences under the applicable authority; detected staleness makes none of
   those decisions. Superseded revisions remain attributable.
5. Record assessed coverage, uninspected dependent-home limitations, unresolved
   effects and each proposed recheck. Reconcile an uncertain update before retry.
   A partial assessment supplies no global propagation or erasure guarantee.

Methods/settings adoption is distinct from a content correction and never rewrites
wiki content. Current operation access, information-use basis and update authority
remain hard prerequisites under the common [binding contract](../workspace-bindings/contract.md).
