# Consult-closure output contract 0.1.0

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Use one default mode, with closure/cancellation and requested later-outcome assessment
as ordinary variants. Related entities may be sections of one useful note. Use
actual authorized tools and homes for any separately requested operations; preparing
a note is not an operation, decision, transfer or acceptance mechanism.

## Inputs

Bind the ordinary request, reader/purpose/use basis and actual assessment time;
exact permitted baseline/change/deliverable/status and report-content references;
source-authored engagement-specific role appointments and attributable review,
release, submission, acceptance, resolution, conclusion, cancellation and closure
acts. Retain commercial conditions and exact pre-agreed procedures, remaining
obligations, continuity/exit terms, every material owner's reuse grants, optional
objectives/measures and correction/uncertain-effect evidence. Sources keep author,
home, passage, coverage, actual time/order and digest. Same-day dependencies need
explicit order. A label alone never establishes exact subject correspondence.

## Output

Return a readable Closure identity/revision and actual disposition; an unsaved note
remains `prepared_unretained`. A structured companion may preserve the same meanings
and exact identities but is no mandatory host schema. Include:

- Exact original promise/baseline and adopted-change/order history. Reconcile differing
  plan, change and deliverable baselines explicitly; no output silently supersedes another.
- Attributable business-event evidence, effective role/authority and exact subject
  lineage. Keep current-version acceptance and historical decisions distinct; unresolved
  contradictions need attributable supersession rather than display-order selection.
- Reservations with criterion/version, resolution owner/date, readiness consequence
  and supported actual disposition. Resolution on a new version cannot rewrite earlier
  acceptance or erase an earlier criterion gap.
- Invoice readiness under the exact commercial condition, invoiced, paid and achieved
  outcome as separate evidence-backed facts. Unknown agreement/resolution evidence
  leaves readiness unknown; no invoice or payment is performed or inferred.
- Conclusion/cancellation/closure, current work boundary, remaining obligations,
  at-risk costs, assigned surviving follow-through and settled versus pending exit.
  Cancellation stops new delivery immediately; cleanup extends no ended authority.
- Permitted complete learning candidate or blocked reuse, follow-on owner/action/date
  or unknown, and exact audience/purpose/use bounds. No opportunity is opened automatically.
- Requested observed/assessed/not-assessable outcomes with actual sources, comparability
  and attribution limits. No measure is mandatory where none was named.
- Human originals/dissent, late objections, exact dependencies, stale support, proposed
  rechecks, unread homes and unresolved effects. Preserve prior outputs and decisions.
- Separate exact-version author checks, actual reader evidence or labeled simulation,
  intended-reader navigation, independent review, actual operation/readback, human
  usefulness and acceptance. Leave unperformed checks `NOT_RUN`.

## Acceptance and commercial fidelity

A named lead is not automatically the accepting party. Rework/rejection/withdrawal
and later objections remain separate from acceptance history; rework creates a new
version. Silence establishes no acceptance. Deemed acceptance requires the exact
pre-agreed procedure, subject/delivery receipt, elapsed period and affirmative
no-response coverage with supported chronology. Attribute its result to the procedure,
never an invented client sign-off, and preserve later objections.

A prior due date or concluded change is historical evidence, not current completion,
usage or permission for new work. Keep original and supplementary work limits,
remaining cleanup and at-risk costs separate. Closure needs attributable authority
and settled continuity/exit arrangements; open obligations can survive only with
explicit assignment. Agreement on retention/export/deletion/departed access does
not establish those operations occurred.

## Learning, outcomes and recovery

Client work stays in its agreed home. Before any reusable contribution, inspect
complete outgoing text, names, metadata, links, comments, attachments and receipts
against every owner's current exact audience/purpose grant. A short anonymous lesson
grant authorizes no dossier, licensed benchmark or reference call. Unknown, revoked
or expired grants and uninspectable surfaces block the affected reuse. Preparation
saves or transfers nothing; denied material contributes no outgoing inventory.

Requested outcome assessment compares named data-owner observations with supplied
objectives/baselines/targets. State units, population, denominator, periods, missingness,
comparability and attribution. Missing is not zero; incompatible or unsupported
measures remain not assessable with needed input/owner. Observed improvement alone
proves no causality, savings or group adoption. Schedule nothing.

Check whole-source meaning, exact subjects, chronology and readable criterion coverage.
On resumption inspect current record, source bytes, late edits and uncertain effects
before retry. An unreadable authoritative record blocks dependent resumption without
a duplicate. Preserve issued history and human originals; report affected checks and
unread dependency coverage without persisting stale flags or blindly retrying a save.
