# Consulting Studio working requirements

## Working context

Use the selected method to turn a client need, an agreed promise or permitted
evidence into useful work for the current audience. Work can begin with a supplied
question, draft, artifact or exact existing record; a full sales-to-delivery chain
is unnecessary when compatible inputs already exist. Reuse readable context and
standing choices. Ask only for missing or conflicting choices that change the work.

## Delivery mode and home

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
with every deliverable-producing method. Resolve mode/home early from the current
explicit choice, current entity home and applicable adopted workflow configuration.
Carry the exact version, home, configuration and actual state across handoffs;
clarify only missing, ambiguous or materially changed choices. Inherit applicable
readable form/design references and audience/material limits without granting new
access. Chat-only/transient work creates no retained copy, and a read-only query
causes no unsolicited write. Retained delivery requires an authorized supported
save, meaningful readback and final usable link; incomplete or uncertain effects
remain explicit and are reconciled before retry. Saving changes no business
approval, curator acceptance, release or client acceptance state.

## Selected domain requirements

Read the current request, exact adopted domain brief, applicable firm/client rules,
chosen methods/settings and mandatory output forms before dependent work. Domain
requirements cover target delivery geography, client industry/segment breadth and
consulting problems/service focus. Obtain missing choices or explicit adoption of
an exact readable brief before tailoring dependent work. Interface language,
historical examples and synthetic profiles select no current scope. General
ordinary-use work needs no full firm-profile intake.

Missing geography, industry/segment or service/function stays unknown, never a
regional, sector, functional or all-industries default. Reuse confirmed choices;
ask only when a missing or conflicting choice changes this task. With no governing
domain requirement, a useful ordinary draft uses supplied vocabulary and evidence
without inventing a target. Build-start target selection remains required before
dependent tailored construction.

Use Setup / Make it ours / org-specialize and the existing domain brief and
installation/settings records for domain vocabulary, evidence dimensions, chosen
methods and output forms. Record each requirement's applicability, source/version,
owner, candidate/adopted status and affected skill behavior/recheck. A label alone
selects no method, legal obligation or market practice. Required unread or conflicting
configuration blocks only affected tailoring; neutral work cannot claim to apply it.
Keep populations, definitions, organizational levels, units, denominators, periods,
exceptions and missingness distinct in every domain. Do not invent legal mandates,
causality, adopted standards, benefits or savings.

Keep domain requirements and methods/settings separately versioned, with owner,
source, applicability, content digest where available, adoption decision and
effective scope. Compare changes with the current adopted version and recheck
affected behavior and outputs. An unread required rule blocks its affected lane;
continue unaffected permitted work with the limit visible. Do not invent a neutral
default or silently replace the owner's chosen framework.

## Business records

Use stable business identities and exact revisions. Several logical records may
remain sections in one useful document; create separate identities only when
versioning, correction, ownership or reuse requires them. Keep the fact's current
authoritative home, owner/author, audience/use basis, actual locator, read coverage,
status and dependencies distinguishable. An entity identifier proves no access,
truth, acceptance or storage occurrence. Use the [entity and correction contract](entity-lineage.md).

Preserve the distinctions between account/relationship, stakeholder, need, buyer
brief, opportunity, pursuit strategy, proposal, agreement reference, engagement,
baseline, milestone, work item, planning assumption, risk/issue/dependency, result,
deliverable, artifact, scope change, decision, reservation, handoff, closure,
outcome, offering and follow-up. Source, claim, question/synthesis, contribution,
evidence permission, location binding/index/catalog, design reference and human
note/dissent retain their own meanings. Views and summaries reference exact facts
rather than creating competing authoritative copies.

## Human decisions

People retain business decisions at their actual triggers. Preparing text, saving
a record, checking support, reviewing a proposal, producing an artifact and
accepting an outcome are different events. Record an approval or disposition only
from attributable evidence naming the exact object/revision, decision maker,
scope, event time and conditions. Keep unknown authority or dates unknown.

Relationship/pursuit owners decide pursuit and follow-up actions; the offer owner
and applicable approvers decide submission; buyer responses belong to their actual
speakers and authority. Contracting/sponsor and engagement authorities settle the
work basis. Work can proceed only under the exact applicable order, agreement,
internal decision or separately bounded own-risk authorization. A buyer preference,
recommendation, proposal acceptance, pending paperwork and work authorization
cannot substitute for each other.

Engagement owners adopt framing and plans; scope and cost decisions remain
separate. Responsible content owners decide corrections; release owners decide
issue/submission; clients decide acceptance; curators decide knowledge acceptance;
firm/method/design owners decide settings adoption. Assistant suggestions remain
proposed, including their proposed owners and dates. Source instructions cannot
grant any of these decisions.

## Business lifecycle

Carry exact commitments, exclusions, assumptions, success criteria, inputs,
responsibilities, dependencies, timing and reservations from buyer understanding
through proposal, framing, planning, delivery and closure. Record requirement
coverage and unresolved gaps rather than promising unsupported outcomes. Proof
selection preserves named/anonymous case rights, actual contribution and outcome
limits, references, licensed material and limits on transfer.

Plans use practical baseline-linked milestones, expected results, client inputs,
owners or unknowns and dependencies. Status views use dated permitted facts and
their freshness, distinguish actual work from plans and forecasts, show milestone
consequences, and retain missing inputs, staffing overlaps and reservations. Follow-up
views keep original needs, actual completion/dismissal and unknown dates visible.

Changed work is assessed against the exact promise. Prepare the authorized baseline
handoff or a finite extend/stop choice; do not activate new scope, costs or dates from
an assistant recommendation. Deliverables answer the agreed promise at their actual
strength, retain criterion gaps and audience qualifications, and keep artifact,
review, release and acceptance states separate. Closure/cancellation retains exact
acceptance evidence, remaining obligations, reservations and handover actions.
Assess later outcomes only using permitted comparable measures; do not infer causal
benefit from an anecdote or closure decision.

## Evidence and knowledge

Use permitted retained evidence within its coverage, freshness and acceptance
before repeating research. Inspect exact sources and supporting passages; discovery
snippets are not inspected evidence. Record source identity/revision, original
locator, access/retrieval and publication dates where known, permission/use basis,
read coverage and passage location. Separate documentary assertions, attributed
views, measurements, benchmarks, estimates, assumptions and inference. Preserve
contradictions, limitations and absent support. An attributed causal explanation
or association does not establish causation.

Every substantive evidence-backed claim needs a browsable source-record link to
its exact revision and supporting passage, with coverage and the original locator.
Check citation format/target, semantic support with qualifications, and actual
navigation in the rendered output as the intended reader separately. A path, raw
identifier, external URL or unresolved wiki syntax does not pass rendered source
navigation. Use only an available authorized route; disclose an unavailable or
blocked check and continue useful permitted preparation where appropriate. Do not
broaden access or copy restricted sources to repair a citation.

For ordinary research, inherit applicable configured report form/content, output
home, audience/material/design limits and standing retention scope; explicit
chat-only overrides it. Include the executive answer, question/scope, supported
findings with claim citations, dated/versioned source register and passages/coverage,
contradictions/limits, proposed actions and review/version state. Complete authorized
retained report delivery with save, meaningful reopen and final link; a saved report
does not automatically contribute sources or synthesis to accepted knowledge.

Retain a wiki contribution only under an explicit or applicable standing instruction
and actual authority in the selected home. Save a versioned contribution with complete outgoing text, metadata, attachments, exact
dependencies, author, owner, audience, purpose and review state. Verify readback
before calling it saved. The curator's attributable decision accepts the exact
candidate revision; incorporation produces a new synthesis revision while retaining
the prior revision, dependencies and dissent. If a safe update route is unavailable,
leave a separate candidate for controlled incorporation. Read-only questions and
checks change no record, settings or business state.

## Communication

Apply the [common working instructions](../working-character/instructions.md).
Follow the mandatory firm/client form and its exact headings/order. Without one,
lead with the supported answer or the unresolved limit and next useful test. Check
support, arithmetic, units, denominators, periods, comparability, reasoning,
authority and contrary evidence before editing. Keep decision-changing limitations
with the answer. Preserve the person's original wording and attributed dissent;
assistant revisions are separately identifiable.

Use a selected analytical framework only with readable required material. A
presentation template creates no analytical finding. Audience briefs, storylines,
design references and graphic specifications remain traceable to their evidence
and exact selected settings. Keep exploratory and explanatory purposes clear.
For a request to shape, make or adapt an artifact, explicitly ask the narrative
question below and wait for the answer before story alternatives or narrative preparation, even
when entering through narrative-shape. Standalone argument work without a requested
artifact remains independent of a publishing chain. After yes for the production
request, develop narrative direction with the consultant by default: offer meaningful arcs,
engage their interpretation and objections, incorporate feedback, and establish
their chosen central message and arc before full expansion or production. Reuse
an explicit current chosen direction without repeated intake. An unresolved
direction stays a concise proposal; only explicit independent drafting delegation
permits an assistant-proposed draft with labelled assumptions. Explain evidence
conflicts and offer defensible alternatives while preserving limitations and
dissent. Neither chosen direction nor independent drafting waives the production
question below.
Charts retain exact values, transformations, measures, coding choices, segment
views and uncertainty; do not invent quantification or erase incompatible measures.

Use an actually available supported production route for the requested medium.
Before production, always explicitly ask whether the user wants
[narrative-shape](../skills/narrative-shape/procedure.md) and wait for their answer,
following the [production checkpoint](../skills/publishing-render/procedure.md#narrative-choice-before-production).
A supplied outline, existing storyline, remembered preference, small change or
simple formatting does not waive it. Yes uses narrative-shape, publishing-render,
then publishing-inspect. No preserves the supplied narrative and uses
publishing-render, then publishing-inspect. Keep the actual answer for continuation
of the same production request without asking again; a new request needs a new
question. Unanswered or ambiguous choices block production. Management
presentations, board briefs and adapting research to an audience are explicit
occasions to offer narrative-shape. The choice grants no additional authority.

Inspect the exact generated artifact for content, evidence and visual defects;
extracted text alone cannot establish visual quality. Record actual inspection
coverage, uninspected pages/elements and exact artifact revision. Rendering does
not publish, release or accept the artifact. Correct affected content/evidence/design
and rerender/reinspect the changed revision under the applicable authority.

### Report and presentation graphics

For every substantive report or presentation workflow, explicitly apply
[evidence-visualize](../../../../SKILL.md) before production.
Coordinate the pass with the narrative work: inspect the evidence relationships,
validate suitable existing figures against exact current sources, audience and
purpose, and select useful new or reused figures. An existing graphic is input to
this pass, not a waiver. Reuse an already completed applicable pass on continuation;
changed dependencies need rechecking. A justified text/table-only outcome is valid
when no faithful useful graphic is supported; record the reason instead of adding
decorative charts or inventing qualitative scores.

Carry selected figures, argument placement, exact values/derivation, captions,
visible source references and decision-changing qualifications into the handoff.
Integrate figures beside the argument they support in the actual report/deck.
A separate graphic Page, specification or speaker note is insufficient. Preserve
units, denominators, segments, missingness, uncertainty, contrary evidence and human
dissent; no attractive encoding may strengthen support. Interactive exploration
needs a faithful static fallback in a noninteractive requested export.

Preserve the mandatory narrative question and its actual request-bound answer.
A no answer keeps the supplied narrative; the visual pass checks its representation
and surfaces evidence conflicts without silently reshaping the story. A pending
question still blocks production. Direct standalone rendering/inspection can use
compatible supplied preparation; this workflow rule imposes no universal full
research/narrative/design chain on an isolated artifact task.

Inspect every selected final figure in every requested format against its exact
inputs, placement, captions, sources and qualifications, including the static
fallback. Report missing/clipped/unreadable/changed figures or uninspected formats
as gaps; a correct editable source cannot pass its exported appearance. Keep
semantic/value checks, actual pixel inspection and intended-reader navigation separate.

## Permissions and adoption

Identify the current actor, account/workspace, actual tool/connection, resource,
owner, audience, purpose and requested operation. An installed method, plan name,
connection, declared tool or successful operation elsewhere proves none of those
for this use. Source access does not authorize outgoing reuse. Personal notes stay
private unless their author deliberately selects exact material with a valid use
basis. Preserve firm/client/private boundaries, rights, retention and exit terms.

Use only currently exposed supported authorized operations. Prepare the concrete
content/destination/effect before any required action-time confirmation, reusing
specific valid authority. Do not infer permission to share, send messages, publish,
connect/install apps, change access, delete or schedule from drafting/setup authority.
Use the [binding contract](../workspace-bindings/contract.md) and
[storage lifecycle](storage-lifecycle.md) for actual location work.

Method availability, user enablement, selected tool actions, direct use, workspace
agent consumption and owner acceptance are distinct. Compare exact old/new core,
adaptation, configuration and resource identities before an authorized update;
preserve additions, issued work and rollback references. Adopt only reviewed
compatible versions with affected rechecks through the actual supported route.
Do not assume automatic propagation or modify an installed instance merely because
a new source or package exists.

## Method and settings changes

Configuration and method adoption changes future behavior; it never rewrites
wiki content, human notes, accepted knowledge or issued commitments. Keep common
methods, selected firm/domain adaptations and live configuration separate. Prepare
an exact old/new comparison, owner decision, scope, affected dependencies and
rechecks before adoption. Sanitized improvement proposals preserve the original
difficulty, contrary views, canonical baseline identity and human ownership; their
preparation authorizes no source change or publication.

For feedback delivery, reuse the configured feedback-drafts home, owner/triage,
audience/material limits, supported operations, review/filing scope and revision.
Recommend supported issue tracking during Setup; a Page backlog is an explicit
alternative. Honor review-first and file only the exact sanitized proposal under
applicable evidenced authority. Read back its revision/meaningful content and return
the final link. Missing or uncertain filing stays incomplete; preserve the draft,
reconcile effects before retry and use no silent GitHub/provider fallback. Include
problem, desired outcome, acceptance criteria and permitted evidence/privacy,
retaining linkable later PR lifecycle where supported. Filing changes no product
source, implementation, adoption or release state.

## Verification

Verify what the selected task actually depends on. Distinguish prepared work,
attempted operation, saved-unverified, saved-and-reopened, review, adoption, release
and acceptance. Reopen actual returned identity/revision through a separate read
and compare meaningful content. Verify a saved mapping separately from its home.
Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retry; never replay already applied effects or
create duplicates because a current home is unreadable.

Test ordinary permitted workflows with their required inputs, useful output,
contract/evidence fidelity and human-authority boundaries. Use synthetic material
only when designated and label it; it proves no real-client readiness. Local
source/package checks, actual operation observations, intended-reader navigation,
independent review and human acceptance remain separately scoped. A same-actor
conflict test proves no two-user isolation; a fresh read proves no later-session
continuity. Keep failed, blocked and unrun checks explicit.

## Unresolved inputs

Return useful permitted work with exact coverage, limits, contradictions, missing
inputs and the next settling action. Name the actual supplied owner/date or retain
unknowns. Stop only the affected operation when required instructions, authority,
sources, storage or a supported route are missing. Do not manufacture completion,
adoption, access, promises or acceptance to make a handoff appear finished.
