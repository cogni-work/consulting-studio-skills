# Take over the promise and frame the work

Read and apply the [common working instructions](references/source/implementation/working-character/instructions.md)
and the [domain requirements](references/source/implementation/runtime/requirements.md#selected-domain-requirements)
selected for this task. Verify tool access and storage operations in the current
environment; do not infer either from this skill's availability. Missing required
instructions or access block only dependent operations; continue permitted work
from supplied material.

Return one useful brief with identifiable Engagement, Baseline, Handoff,
Stakeholder and expected Outcome sections where applicable. Preparation records no
actual adoption, start, retention, release or acceptance. People retain those decisions.

Read the [output contract](references/source/implementation/skills/consult-framing/references/output-contract.md), [operational requirements](references/source/implementation/skills/consult-framing/references/source-map.md),
[entity lineage](references/source/implementation/runtime/entity-lineage.md), [binding contract](references/source/implementation/workspace-bindings/contract.md)
and this method's entity-home/method-settings roles in the
[binding catalog](references/source/implementation/runtime/binding-catalog.json). Resolve each needed
operation against the actual authorized home and available tools. Reuse current
valid target choices, authority, answered questions and standing effort limits.
No full firm setup is required.

1. Identify the ordinary request, purpose, intended readers, exact engagement and
   current home. Use the smallest sufficient readable input. Preserve original
   wording, notes/dissent, source revisions, passages, read coverage and rights.
   Synthetic/sanitized work needs no real-client setup. Before admitting real
   material, establish authorized assistance/context/audience and applicable policy.
   Access, information use, operational acceptance and work authority are separate.
   Unread/private/restricted items stay excluded from text, links, metadata and receipts.
2. Choose the compatible entry:
   - **sale-handover:** inspect the exact accepted proposal when present, submission
     and acceptance evidence, current order/call-off and handoff. Carry question,
     promises, scope/exclusions, staffing/price assumptions, stakeholders and risks
     with their existing qualifications. Delivery lead accepts or names gaps. When
     seller is lead, an attributable acknowledgement suffices: reference rather
     than retype the promise. Adopt criteria already agreed without redundant approval.
     A direct award has no invented proposal; the actual call-off supplies its promise.
   - **existing-framing:** adopt the readable exact agreed question/scope/criteria and
     decision references. No sale, pursuit or handoff is forced. Preserve stable
     baseline identity and revision rather than create a competing current baseline.
     A delivery lead adopting existing framing is not thereby a seller or a receiver
     acknowledging a sale handoff; attribute only the roles evidenced in this context.
   - **internal-work:** inspect the bounded internal/investment/pro-bono decision,
     sponsor, purpose, scope, caps/expiry and criteria. No buyer, proposal or order is
     invented. A workshop's criterion can be observable participation/result rather
     than a fabricated rendered file.
3. Check the authority at the requested work assessment, against whole original
   context, actual role/subject and current limits/consumption. An accepted proposal,
   buyer yes, framework or NDA alone authorizes no paid work. One effective primary
   basis covers permitted work: concluded order with concluded amendments, or internal
   decision. Preserve earlier authority and supersession dates without backdating;
   separately bound any own-risk supplement. A historical concluded order remains
   historical even if present work coverage is unproved. For bounded sources apply
   the current [time/authority procedure](references/source/implementation/skills/sales-pursuit-event/references/time-authority-model-0.1.15.md).
   A responsible usage report neither grants nor extends authority; unknown balance,
   expiry, revocation, suspension/cancellation or exhausted caps blocks dependent work.
4. Separate agreed framing from proposals and gaps. Identify question/optional
   structure, in/out/adjacent scope, dates, success criteria, accepting party/procedure
   and exact version to be reviewed. Record expected outcomes only when named, with
   supplied measure/target/baseline, data owner and later assessment date or unknown.
   Delivery acceptance is distinct from measured business effect. Missing criteria,
   method or unsupported extra scope stays unresolved with owner/settling evidence;
   do not invent a business/legal decision to complete a template.
   Carry agreement status through the wording inside every section: a proposed
   acceptance procedure/home remains proposed. An assessment timestamp is not a work
   start date or bounded authority interval when no such authority is supplied.
5. Identify effective method/settings versions and their content evidence, applicability
   and attributable choice. Missing required methods block their tailored lane; common
   framing continues. Preserve supplied definitions, organizational levels, populations,
   units, periods and exceptions; apply domain vocabulary and evidence requirements
   only from applicable configuration or supplied evidence. Labels establish no
   legal mandate, adopted standard or savings.
   Method adoption changes no notes, issued promises, authority or wiki content.
6. Reference agreed home/category/context, information owner, lead, permitted
   participants, continuity owner and exit recipient/retention/export/access terms.
   Reuse valid arrangements. Durable client work or colleague reuse needs those terms
   and demonstrated operations/access; missing proof blocks that operation, not a
   permitted unretained brief. No quiet destination fallback or automatic firm reuse.
7. Lead with what can be adopted/prepared and what cannot proceed. Keep material
   exclusions, competing views and decision-changing unknowns beside their claims.
   Give planning the baseline commitments, result criteria and client-input needs
   with owners/dates or unknown and consequences; do not fabricate assignments or
   invoke planning/analysis. Return exact source links/qualifications and proposed
   next settling questions. Readable sections and structured evaluation agree.
8. Self-check wording against whole evidence, including summary claims and numbers.
   Check source/version/passage links separately from semantic support and actual
   intended-reader wiki navigation. Record reader misunderstandings honestly: simulated
   author checks are neither human usefulness nor independent review. Changed bytes
   invalidate affected checks; identify all readable affected dependencies and unknown
   coverage. Preserve prior outputs, human originals and authority history. On resumption
   reconcile exact inputs/configuration/current record and uncertain effects before retry;
   create no replacement record. Stop at the selected scope for independent review.
