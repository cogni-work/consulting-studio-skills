# Current pursuit time and authority procedure 0.1.15

Read this procedure before assessing current bounded-work coverage. Apply it with
the [skill](../procedure.md), [event contract](event-contract.md), common bindings and
the [current consumption policy](time-authority-model-0.1.15.md).
Preserve historical acts, original evidence, unknowns, rights, human originals and
dissent. A current assessment never rewrites a historical grant or business event.

1. Prove the whole actual fact and source context: actor, exact grant/work/purpose,
   complete passage and permitted original. Draft, quotation, hypothetical,
   conditional, denied, mixed or unknown context supplies no positive authority.
2. Prove the reporter's responsibility for that exact authorization at measurement.
   A whole current original acting sponsor's responsible-person report may carry
   an attributable attestation; mere approval identity does not. A different
   reporter needs actual applicable responsibility, or a whole attributable
   responsible-person attestation. Labels and copied metadata alone supply none.
3. Keep actual act, issue, knowledge, role-effective interval, consumption
   measurement and work assessment separate. Future/expired responsibility alone
   is nonproof. An additional future appointment does not cancel current proof;
   explicit current denial or exclusive replacement remains decisive.
4. Prove the original continuing bounded grant: purpose/work/parties/risk bearer,
   currency, caps, effective date, expiry and revocation. Consumption reports and
   continuity statements neither grant, reinstate nor extend permission.
5. Establish supported current balance. Classify evidence as **proof**, **nonproof
   context**, or **contradiction**. An agreeing report with unestablished reporter
   responsibility supplies no proof and does not erase an independently supported
   balance. Every actual report still undergoes full context, lineage, measurement,
   rights and numeric checks; incompatible totals, unknown consumption, applicable
   current denial or revocation block. Older positive evidence cannot hide later
   contrary usage. Selected reports used to establish coverage must supply responsibility proof.
   A faithful blocked record may retain a whole unproved/denied report without
   conferring coverage; documentary correspondence remains mandatory.

Inspect the whole documentary context rather than matching wording alone.
Unsupported paraphrases require source or human clarification. Actual business
authority, operational release, information-use permission and technical access
remain separate. Author checks cannot establish independent review, actual
operation or business acceptance.
