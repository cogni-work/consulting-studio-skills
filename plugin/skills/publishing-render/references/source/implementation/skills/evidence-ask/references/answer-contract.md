# Retained answer contract 0.2.1

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Return the user's question and a useful answer at the actual evidence strength.
The readable answer preserves material qualifications, attribution, disagreements,
coverage limits and the next unresolved decision. A formal tree, a full wiki setup
or a new firm-profile interview is unnecessary for an already answerable question.

For reused knowledge identify the exact accepted Question, Synthesis and Claim
revisions, supporting Source revisions/passages, and attributable curator decision
(subject revisions, curator, date, disposition and scope). Preserve immutable
accepted history. A separately readable correction can invalidate current use of
an older dependency without silently rewriting the accepted synthesis. A current
unaccepted synthesis or contrary draft stays provisional, even when useful.
Acceptance of a source as a record of a proposal is not adoption of that proposal.
Knowledge acceptance, independent truth, scoped human usefulness, governance/client
approval, retention and release are separate decisions.

Record source dates/periods (unknown remains unknown), actual read coverage and
omissions, present actor/audience/purpose read and reuse rights, and each gap's
settling evidence or test. Do not inherit the previous researcher's original-source
inspection. Reading retained passages establishes passage coverage only. Synthesis
access does not establish source access; readable synthesis-only material needs its
actual narrower basis and unresolved citation readiness. Generic access limitations
must not enumerate denied/private inventory. Denied/revoked use remains blocked;
permitted independent work can continue without moving restricted material.

Preserve human original wording and supplied author/version/passage alongside
assistant interpretation. Late shared dissent remains attributable and unaccepted
until its own disposition; unselected private notes never become shared by default.
Do not average disagreement, treat absent evidence as refutation, or assign unknown
human authorities/owners/dates. Question agreement, responsibility assignments,
proposal adoption and release remain decisions for the relevant people.

Each evidence-backed substantive statement (including summary and numeric claims)
and inference needs complete traceable supporting passages and cited inputs.
Citations target authorized browsable source records with exact version/passage
identification; raw IDs or original external URLs alone are insufficient. Keep
format/target checks, semantic passage support and actual rendered navigation as the
intended reader separately scoped. If actual navigation was not observed,
it stays not run/incomplete, even if local source-record targets correspond exactly.

Query mode changes no record, grant, settings, stale flag, contribution or catalog.
The answer is unretained unless a separate authorized retention action occurs.
A follow-up research/retention request resolves its own destination and current
rights. No silent mutation or automatic compounding follows a query.

Use a structured sidecar only when useful for the requested handoff; it is not a
mandatory response or an access enforcement mechanism. Mechanical checks cannot independently certify full
claim coverage, semantic truth, usefulness, human authority or actual navigation;
exact-output independent review and the applicable human decision remain separate.
