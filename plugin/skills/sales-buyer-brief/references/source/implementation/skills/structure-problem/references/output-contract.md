# Problem structure output contract 0.1.0

Apply [Deliverable mode and home](../../../runtime/storage-lifecycle.md#deliverable-mode-and-home)
early and carry its selected mode/home, exact version and actual state into the
handoff. This does not expand read-only/preparation scope or business authority;
a retained copy needs its own applicable instruction and save/readback/final link.

Return a readable versioned scoping note: question and reframe trail; our-reading
or evidenced agreed status; parts; in/out/adjacent scope; settling tests and research
handoff; evidence/requirement/method limits; next decision and author self-checks.
Human originals remain separate and exact. A draft establishes no saved business record.

The handoff retains stable structure/question/P/RQ/CI IDs and revisions. Each part
has a named cutting principle, open/supported/refuted status and basis, exact
source/version/passage references, supported-if/refuted-if tests, analysis and client
inputs with owner/date or unknown. State overlaps, omissions and answer combination.
Preserve conflicting definitions and measures rather than inventing reconciliation.

When a structured handoff is useful, retain these fields alongside the readable note:

- `skill`, `version`, `case_id`, `structure_id`, `structure_revision`, `status`,
  `question` (original, proposed, subject, decision, horizon), `cutting_principle`.
- `parts`: `id`, `status`, `basis`, `citations`, `rq_ids`, `ci_ids`, `supported_if`,
  `refuted_if`, `analysis`, `inputs` (id, need, owner, due).
- `scope` (`in`, `out`, `adjacent`, each explicitly proposed/agreed and reason),
  `overlaps_omissions`, `answer_combination`, `framework` (choice/status/reference),
  `dissent` (source identity and exact supplied text), `requirements`, `limitations`,
  `next_decision`, `checks` and `dispositions`.

A citation is `{source_id, version, passage}`. A downstream research answer retains
these exact handoff identities; direct research instead records its supplied question
and does not fabricate an upstream execution. A structured sidecar is optional;
these meanings do not prescribe a user interface or platform schema.

Format/reference correspondence proves neither passage truth nor wiki navigation.
Record actual workflow operation, collaboration and real-client readiness separately.
