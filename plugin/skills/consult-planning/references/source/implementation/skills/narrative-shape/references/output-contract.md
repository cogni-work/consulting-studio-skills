## Direction and collaboration

For a request to shape, make or adapt an artifact, complete the explicit narrative
question and wait at this method's entry, before story alternatives or narrative
preparation; do not defer it to publishing-render. Reuse the actual question and
answer on continuation of that same request; a new request needs its own question
and answer. After yes, develop direction below. After no, preserve the supplied
narrative for rendering/inspection without narrative-shape. An unanswered or
ambiguous choice remains pending. Standalone argument work without a requested
artifact remains independent of the production checkpoint and publishing chain.

Co-creation is the default. An ordinary presentation request or a yes to the
narrative method does not delegate independent drafting. Begin with the
consultant's interpretation and audience response, offer a provisional reading,
and use a focused question. Propose materially different arcs where a meaningful
choice remains, with their central messages, logic, evidence, objections,
tradeoffs and limits. Invite the consultant's own alternative. Preserve actual
feedback and show how it changed the message or arc; rejection requires a
substantive revision rather than cosmetic retitling.

The working brief and handoff distinguish consultant input, assistant proposals,
actual feedback, incorporated changes, the consultant's chosen central message
and story arc with rationale, and unresolved choices. Establish both message and
arc before full expansion or production; an unresolved direction stays a concise
proposal awaiting the consultant. Silence, ambiguity, document headings and a
simulated reader check establish no consultant choice. Reuse an explicit current
chosen direction without repeated intake or arc menus, unless changed evidence,
audience, purpose or constraints require an affected revision.

Explicitly requested independent drafting, including a readable explicit delegation
applicable to this work, permits an assistant-proposed draft with labelled
assumptions and open decisions. Identify its scope and actual basis; do not call it
consultant-chosen or fabricate dialogue. An ordinary production request or inferred
preference supplies no delegation. It does not waive the mandatory production
question or grant evidence, storage, publication or business authority.

If the preferred argument conflicts with the evidence, explain the limitation and
offer defensible narrower claims, open questions or alternative arcs. Preserve
contrary findings and human dissent; preference cannot strengthen evidence.

## Prepared narrative and handoff

Each output contains versioned Audience Brief and Storyline sections, a structure
rationale, supported close, objections and explanation-depth choices; substantive
claim inventory with kind/strength, qualifications and exact passage references;
read coverage, dissent and exclusions; unfamiliar-reader findings and actual test
scope; production handoff when requested; operation/review/acceptance dispositions.
At an unresolved direction stage, return only the useful brief, alternative arcs,
feedback incorporated, pending choice and next focused question; do not fabricate
a complete Storyline or reader-test result. Under explicit independent delegation,
label the full draft's proposed direction and assumptions separately from human
choice or acceptance.
Fit the chosen arc within the required form's exact headings and order. Its beats
belong within the permitted sections; choosing an arc does not replace the form or
authorize renaming or reordering mandatory headings. The prepared sections above
do not override that form. Surface a real conflict for the responsible owner.
Distinguish supplied action, suggested settling test and decision already taken.
No unsupported action/urgency/causality or approval may enter the argument.

Audience targeting changes argument, language and emphasis while conserving the
underlying supported propositions. Preserve exact permitted human dissent rather
than silently paraphrasing the original. Summaries, prefaces and handoffs preserve
the original subject, time and scope of reservation: a readiness or timing objection
supplies no wider position on the proposal without evidence. Numeric comparisons
identify population, units, denominator, period and missingness; incompatible populations stay separate.

Every evidence-backed unit maps to exact permitted Source/Result revision, content
digest and passage with quoted support and a readable record link. Record/anchor
correspondence is separate from semantic support and actual rendered wiki navigation
as the intended reader. Local links are preparation only: missing authorized
wiki/production routes leave those
checks NOT_RUN. Source records expose quality, read coverage, quoted passages and
original locator where supplied; never invent an external original or author.

Reader checks bind the content digest and report conclusion/question, material
limit and actual action comprehension, misunderstandings and concrete changes.
Reader findings, visualization rationales and handoffs describe only content actually
present and inspected in their declared scope; distinguish proposed tables, figures
or additions from completed work. Changed tested content
makes its findings stale until an actual recheck; a refreshed digest alone is no recheck.
Simulation, author check, independent source review, named human review, release
and client acceptance have separate dispositions at exact versions.

Production handoff binds exact content/evidence/qualifications and audience/purpose,
design revision/digest when supplied, requested medium, intended relationship and
underlying values/derivation where relevant. Receipt of a compatible handoff is
not rendering, acceptance or authorization to publish. A requested deck can remain
an explicitly incomplete outline; do not create a duplicate rendering engine.
It also binds the direction state: the actual consultant-chosen message/arc and
feedback incorporated, or the scoped explicit independent delegation and
assistant-proposed direction. A merely proposed unchosen arc cannot silently
become production content. Retain open decisions, evidence limits and dissent.

For a production request, retain its explicit narrative question and actual answer
with the handoff. Management presentations, board briefs and adapting research to
an audience are discovery triggers, not permission to skip the question. A yes
answer selects narrative-shape before publishing-render and publishing-inspect;
a no answer preserves the supplied narrative and bypasses narrative preparation.
The answer remains valid for continuation of that same request. An unanswered or
ambiguous checkpoint remains pending; prior preferences supply no answer for a new
request. The choice grants no additional access, retention or publication authority.

Correction binds prior and replacement bytes even at an unchanged revision label.
Enumerate known content/evidence dependencies transitively, proposed stale support,
affected narrative/check/handoff versions, revised drafts and required rechecks.
Keep issued text and human originals fixed, disclose incomplete dependency coverage
and distinguish read-only proposed impacts from owner-authorized saved/read-back flags.

Evidence reports pin procedure/contract version, governing source, exact input/output
bytes, actual environment, contributor roster and observed checks. Label synthetic
inputs and simulated checks explicitly. Do not infer current target adoption,
access enforcement, rendering, wiki navigation, independent review or owner
acceptance from a prepared narrative or mechanical correspondence checks.

## Structured handoff

Alongside the readable reply, return these fields; use null or `unknown` rather than
invent. The readable reply keeps the configured form's sections and states the same
delivery mode/state, configuration, questions, storyline identity, uncertain writes
and untrusted passages. Controlled values are exact.

`storyline.sha256` and `production_handoff.content.sha256` bind the complete UTF-8
readable reply exactly as delivered, including its preface, all sections, review
text, whitespace and final newline. Keep these digest values in the machine
companion, outside the hashed readable text. `reader_check.tested_sha256` identifies
that exact reply version; separately describe the actual inspected scope, questions,
observations and findings. Matching the reply digest does not establish that every
part was tested or understood. A meaningful reader check still addresses the
conclusion or open question, material qualification and actual decision/action;
semantic review assesses the reported scope and findings. The readable headings
and their order bind the configured form; no list-only representation of
`storyline.sections` is required.

Each citation includes `source_id`, `revision`, `passage` and `quote`. Resolve its
identity/revision to an actually selected source or selected human-note record.
`quote` is a verbatim excerpt of that exact passage; ellipsis-separated fragments
must remain in passage order. A prior conversation's chosen-turn reference belongs
to direction metadata, not a fabricated Source record.

| Field | Content and controlled values |
| --- | --- |
| `request_id` | Production request this turn belongs to |
| `narrative_question` | `request_id`; `status` `asked-this-turn`, `answered-yes`, `answered-no`, `pending` or `not-applicable`; `answer_source` `prior-turn`, `this-turn` or `none`; `asked_this_turn` true/false |
| `direction` | `state` `consultant-chosen`, `delegated`, `proposed` or `pending`; `central_message`; `arc`; `chosen_turn` (turn number or null); consultant input, assistant proposals and feedback incorporated |
| `storyline` | `id`, `version`, `sections`, full-readable-reply `sha256`; null before story work |
| `sources` | Selected permitted records used: `source_id`, `revision`, nonempty actual descriptive `coverage`, quality and `omissions`; supplied omission strings remain a literal subset, with additional actual read limits allowed |
| `claims` | `id`, `wording`, `kind` (`observation`, `attributed`, `inference`, `assumption`, `recommendation`, `open-question`), strength, `qualification`, `citations` of `{source_id, revision, passage, quote}`; evidence-backed kinds cite at least one passage |
| `dissent` | Exact permitted notes: `id`, `version`, `author`, `date`, `passage`, `text`; summaries go elsewhere |
| `withheld` | `denied_count`; `material` of `{ref, reason}` only for selected, request-named unsupplied real-material references; reason `unsupplied`, `out-of-limits` or `untrusted`; no denied identities or source/design-read inventory |
| `delivery` | `mode` `retained`, `chat-only` or `unresolved`; `state` `not-started`, `chat-delivered`, `prepared`, `saved-unverified` or `saved-readback`; `configuration_id`; `configuration_revision`; `configuration_use` `applied`, `overridden` or `needs-choice`; `home` (configured home when retained, else null); `retention_authority` `standing`, `explicit` or `none`; `review_state` `not-requested`, `awaiting-review` or `reviewed`; `design_refs` of `{id, version, conformance}` with `verified`, `unverified` or `not-applicable`; `final_link`; `prior_writes` of `{attempt_id, status, retried}` with status `unreconciled`, `reconciled-present` or `reconciled-absent`; `fallback_used` |
| `questions_asked` | Every question put to the user this turn, verbatim |
| `visualization` | `outcome` `figures`, `text-table-only` or `not-applicable`; `rationale` |
| `production_handoff` | Handoff when production is requested and stage permits it, else null; `request_id`, `content` of `{storyline_id, version, sha256}` identify the exact storyline and full readable reply; optional `claim_ids` and `dissent_ids` refer only to IDs in the corresponding own ledgers; preserve the semantic evidence/audience/direction lineage above |
| `reader_check` | `kind` `simulated`; `tested_sha256` identifies the full reply version; actual inspected scope, questions and findings remain explicit, or null before a check |
| `untrusted_content` | `{source_id, revision, passage, handling}` with handling `not-followed` |
| `effects` | Booleans `saved`, `sent`, `rendered`, `published`, `wiki_written`, `retained_copy`, `provider_write` |
| `dispositions` | `human_review`, `independent_review`, `rendering`, `inspection`, `installed_agent`, `wiki_navigation`, `owner_acceptance`: `not_run` or `not_applicable` unless actually observed |
| `checks`, `limitations`, `next_decision` | Author self-checks actually performed; limits; next decision with owner or unknown |
